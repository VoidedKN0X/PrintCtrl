<p>Your Bambu Lab printers and filament spools, from your self-hosted Bambuddy and Spoolman servers.</p>
<ul>
<li>Live printer status, progress and time left, with temperature graphs</li>
<li>Live camera with full screen, zoom and saving pictures</li>
<li>Print controls: pause, resume, stop, speed, light, fans, temperatures, jog, filament, skip parts</li>
<li>AMS slots with filament and linked Spoolman spool</li>
<li>Queue, file library, server slicing and share-to-print</li>
<li>Print archive with timelapses, statistics and costs per month</li>
<li>Spoolman inventory with weigh-ins, QR and NFC scanning and low-stock alerts</li>
<li>Progress notification (Live Update on Android 16), first-layer check and alerts via polling or ntfy</li>
<li>Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile</li>
<li>Two addresses per server (you pick which one is used) and self-signed certificates trusted by fingerprint</li>
<li>Private on the lock screen; works with Bambuddy, Spoolman or both</li>
</ul>
